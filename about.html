Our mission

eclair is a shared research platform hosted at IPGG, located at 6 Jean Calvin, Paris 5ᵉ. It was created to pool state-of-the-art electrochemical instrumentation and make it accessible to research groups and partners.

We are open to academic collaborations. External access can be arranged on a case-by-case basis.
