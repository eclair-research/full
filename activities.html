About the platform

Our Activities

The Electro Chemical pLatform for educAtion, Innovation and Research hosts a wide range of activities dedicated to research, training, and knowledge transfer.

First, the platform supports cutting-edge research programmes by providing access to state-of-the-art equipment, advanced electrochemical technologies, and unique expertise developed in Paris.

Beyond research activities, the platform aims to disseminate its knowledge and technical skills by welcoming external users interested in learning, developing new methodologies, or implementing their own electrochemical projects.

Therefore, students from various academic backgrounds and levels, as well as professionals seeking advanced training, are invited to benefit from this collaborative environment.

By fostering innovation, knowledge sharing, and scientific discovery, the platform serves as a dynamic hub for the development of tomorrow's electrochemical solutions.
